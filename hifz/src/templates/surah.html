Tips that work

  • Listen first. Play today's ayahs several times with a reciter you like before reading them. Open them on Quran.com.
  • Small, then join. Repeat each ayah until you can say it without looking, then join it to the previous ones.
  • Same mushaf, same layout. Always memorize from the same page layout: your visual memory of the page will help.
  • Recite it in salah. Using newly memorized ayahs in prayer is one of the best revisions.
  • Check with a teacher. A plan organizes your effort; it does not replace a qualified teacher who corrects your tajweed.

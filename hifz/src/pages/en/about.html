About {{site_name}}

A free planner that turns the intention to memorize the Quran into a clear daily routine.

Why this tool

Many people start memorizing with enthusiasm and stop after a few weeks, often because they lose track of what to learn and what to review. Quran teachers solve this with a simple routine: a new lesson (sabaq), revision of recent lessons (sabqi) and rotating revision of older material (manzil). {{site_name}} applies that routine automatically to your goal, your pace and your week.

How the plan is built

  • Your selection is split into daily portions, in ayahs or in mushaf lines (15 per page).
  • Each study day adds a new portion. The portions of the last few study days are reviewed as sabqi.
  • Everything older, plus the juz you already know, is reviewed as manzil in a rotating cycle, in mushaf order.
  • In ayah mode, a daily portion never crosses from one surah to the next.

Data sources

  • Mushaf structure (Madani mushaf, Hafs, 604 pages, juz and surah boundaries): quran-meta library (MIT licence).
  • Surah names in several languages: quran-json by Risan Bagja Pradana (CC BY-SA 4.0).
  • Reading and listening links open Quran.com, an independent website.

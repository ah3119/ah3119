About {{site_name}}

A free notebook to support children's Quran memorization at home.

Why this notebook

Many parents want their children to memorize Juz ʿAmma, but knowing every evening what to learn and what to revise takes organisation few have time for. Quran schools use a simple routine: a new passage, revision of recent passages and rotating revision of older ones. {{site_name}} applies that routine for you, at your child's pace.

How the programme is calculated

  • The notebook follows the ayahs actually recited: what you mark “Recited” is learned; “To repeat” brings the passage back next session.
  • A passage never mixes two surahs.
  • Recent revision covers the passages of the last sessions; old revision rotates through known surahs in mushaf order.
  • Dates shown are estimates, recalculated every day: a missed session never makes you “fail”.

Data sources

  • Reference mushaf: the Madinah Mushaf, Hafs ʿan ʿĀṣim narration, printed by the King Fahd Glorious Qur'an Printing Complex (604 pages, 6,236 ayahs).
  • Surah, ayah and page structure of this mushaf: quran-meta library (MIT licence).
  • Surah names: quran-json by Risan Bagja Pradana (CC BY-SA 4.0).
  • “Listen” links open Quran.com, an independent website.

{{site_name}} organises memorization; it does not replace a teacher who listens to the child and corrects their tajweed.

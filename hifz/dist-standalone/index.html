How it works

  • NewA small passage to learn, often 2 to 5 ayahs (the sabaq).
  • Recent revisionThe passages of the last sessions, until they are solid (the sabqi).
  • Old revisionOne or two known surahs in rotation, so nothing is forgotten (the manzil).

You tick what your child actually recited. If they hesitate, tap “To repeat”: the passage comes back next session. Skip a day and nothing is lost: the plan resumes where you were.

A typical session (10 to 15 minutes)

  1. Open the passage in the Madinah Mushaf (📖 button) and read it together.
  2. Listen to the new passage three times with the “Listen” button.
  3. Repeat ayah by ayah, then link the ayahs together.
  4. Your child recites without looking: “Recited” or “To repeat”.
  5. Do the recent revision, then the old revision.

Tips for parents

  • Short and regular: 10 minutes every day beat an hour at the weekend.
  • Same time: after a prayer or before bedtime. The calendar reminder helps a lot.
  • Encourage: every recited passage is a star, every learned surah is a celebration.
  • Recite in prayer: it is the best revision.

For the teacher

The weekly sheet sums up the planned passages and what was actually recited. Print it or paste it into WhatsApp: the teacher sees at a glance where the child is and can add a note.

This notebook organises memorization; it does not replace a teacher who listens to the child and corrects their recitation (tajweed).

Frequently asked questions

Where is my child's data saved?

Only on your phone. No account, no server. The first name is optional.

I have several children.

Add one plan per child with “+ Add a child”. Each plan has its own progress.

We missed several days.

No problem: today's session resumes exactly where you stopped, and the estimated finish date is recalculated.

I'm changing phones.

In “Settings”, copy the backup link and open it on the new phone.

Which mushaf is the reference?

The Madinah Mushaf in the Hafs ʿan ʿĀṣim narration, printed by the King Fahd Glorious Qur'an Printing Complex: 604 pages and 6,236 ayahs. Ayah numbers and pages match this mushaf, the most widespread in the world.

Is it free?

Yes, free and ad-free during the test phase.

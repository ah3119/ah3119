Sabaq, sabqi and manzil: the method behind the plan

  • SabaqThe new lesson of the day. A small portion you memorize thoroughly.
  • SabqiRevision of the lessons from the last few days, until they are solid.
  • ManzilRotating revision of everything memorized earlier, so nothing is forgotten.

This three-part routine is used in Quran schools around the world. Hifzly simply does the bookkeeping for you: what to learn today, what to review, and when you will finish.

How long does it take to memorize the Quran?

The standard Madani mushaf has 604 pages of 15 lines. Here is how long the new memorization takes at different paces, with 6 study days a week:

New portion per study dayStudy daysDuration (6 days a week)
5 lines1812≈ 5.8 years
7 lines (½ page)1295≈ 4.1 years
10 lines906≈ 2.9 years
1 page (15 lines)604≈ 1.9 years

Revision days and life events add time. Choose a pace you can keep for months: regularity matters more than speed.

Tips

  • Same time every day, ideally after Fajr when the mind is fresh.
  • Listen before you read: use the “Read & listen” link of each lesson.
  • Never skip manzil: new pages are worthless if old ones fade.
  • Recite to a teacher regularly. A planner organizes your effort; it does not correct tajweed.

Frequently asked questions

Which mushaf does Hifzly use?

The standard 15-line Madani mushaf (Hafs ʿan ʿĀṣim), 604 pages. Page numbers match most printed copies of this layout; line numbers are counted 1 to 15 on each page. If you use a 16-line (Indo-Pak) mushaf, choose the ayah mode: surah and ayah references are the same in every mushaf.

Is it suitable for children?

Yes. Use the “Juz Amma for kids” plan or count in ayahs with a small daily portion. Parents can print the plan and tick each day.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only (browser storage). Your plan is also stored in the page link: bookmark it or send it to your teacher.

Yes, free and without sign-up.

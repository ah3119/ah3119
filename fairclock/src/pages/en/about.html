About {{site_name}}

A free planner for teams, friends and families who live in different time zones.

Why we built it

Remote work, freelancing and families spread across continents have made one question part of daily life: when can we all talk? Most tools only convert time zones. They ignore that the weekend is Friday–Saturday in Riyadh and Saturday–Sunday in London, that Diwali, Eid or Thanksgiving empty an office, and that the same colleague always ends up on the 10 p.m. call. {{site_name}} brings these three dimensions together and adds a simple idea: when no time is perfect, share the inconvenience fairly.

How it works

  • Time zones: computed with the IANA time zone database built into your browser, including daylight saving time.
  • Public holidays and weekends: from the open-source holidays library (MIT licence), national calendars for 2026–2028.
  • Cities: from GeoNames (CC BY 4.0), cities above 100,000 inhabitants and all capitals.
  • Privacy: everything runs in your browser. Your team is stored in the share link and, optionally, in your browser's local storage. We have no database of users.

Limits

Regional holidays (for one state or province), company days off and personal leave are not included. Islamic holidays are estimated from the lunar calendar and can move by a day depending on moon sighting. Always confirm important dates with the people involved.
